Welcome to Fish Sort! Finally released, and thus on Version 1! Although it's not much of a "finally", I did this in 4 afternoons! Is this what a Game Jam feels like?!?!

Sort the 4 images of fish given, into their corresponding 1 of 12 fish family groupings, as separated in the REEF Caribbean Fish Guide!
Click the "Start" button to begin the game!
Click an image to select it. Then, click the corresponding family group it is from to sort it.
If you make a mistake, click the "Clear" button to bring all the fish back to their original positions.
Once you've finished and are confident with your sorting, click the "Check" button to check your answers! It will then show you the correct answers using abbreviations.

Thanks to the Thriving Islands' Butler Bay Reef Guide! That is where all the images were taken from. Check them out!
https://www.thrivingislands.com/reefs
